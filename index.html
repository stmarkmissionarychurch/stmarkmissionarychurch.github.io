+Welcome!

It is with great joy that we greet you in the name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. We want you to know that you belong here at St. Mark Missionary Baptist Church in Vienna, Georgia.

Our pastor, Rev. James E. Jordan Sr., leads us in worship and in the work of reaching the lost, making disciples and transforming lives.
